What running against a map actually gets you.

  • Territory capture

    Every synced run outlines new tiles on the map. Re-run a route before someone else does to keep hold of it.

  • Daily missions

    A fresh objective each day — a distance, a pace, sometimes a specific route — with XP for finishing it.

  • Local leaderboards

    Rankings reset weekly and scope to your area, so someone starting today still has ground worth chasing.

  • Strava sync, not a replacement

    REGN runs alongside Strava, reading activity you've already recorded. It doesn't ask you to switch apps mid-run.
