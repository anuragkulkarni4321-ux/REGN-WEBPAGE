Delete Your Account & Data

Last Updated: September 2026

You can request deletion of your REGN account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to request deletion, what gets removed, and how long it takes.

Request deletion

Send an email from the address linked to your REGN account with the subject line "Delete my account". Include the name or email you signed up with so we can locate your account.

Email deletion request

What gets deleted

Data Deleted?
Name and email address Yes
Strava athlete ID and authorization Yes — access is revoked and the ID is removed
Synced running activity data Yes
GPS route information Yes
Territory, XP, missions and leaderboard progress Yes

Timeline

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Some information may be retained for a short additional period where we are required to keep it for security, fraud prevention, or legal reasons, after which it is permanently removed.

Disconnecting Strava without deleting your account

If you only want to stop syncing activities, you can revoke REGN's access from your Strava account settings at any time — this does not delete your existing REGN progress. To remove your REGN data as well, use the deletion request above.
