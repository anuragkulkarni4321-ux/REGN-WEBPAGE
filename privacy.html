Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

REGN respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Strava athlete ID
  • Running activity data you explicitly authorize us to access
  • GPS route information associated with synced activities

How We Use Your Information

  • Sync your running activities from Strava.
  • Generate in-game progress such as territory, XP, missions and rankings.
  • Improve application performance and reliability.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only access Strava information after you explicitly authorize the connection.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect user information. Authentication tokens are securely stored.

Your Rights

You may disconnect your Strava account at any time and request deletion of your account and associated game data.
